The return-load gap

Return trips should carry loads, not empty kilometres.

VahanConnect helps shippers discover available trucks on their route and helps truck owners find return loads.

Why route matching matters

After an interstate delivery, a truck may find no load back and return empty. VahanConnect shows such trucks to shippers on the same route so both sides can negotiate directly.

Illustrative prototype data: the activity feed and counters are simulated.

    How it works

    From route to negotiation in four steps

    1. 1Owner lists route, capacity and availability.
    2. 2Shipper searches by route and load requirement.
    3. 3Shipper compares available options.
    4. 4Shipper contacts the owner and negotiates directly.

    VahanConnect improves discovery before negotiation.

    Booking, payment, live tracking and document verification appear in the prototype only as simulations. They are future functionality, and no safety guarantee is offered.

    Benefits

    Understanding the benefits of VahanConnect

    For truck owners

    Owners can show their available trucks and return routes directly to shippers. This increases their chances of getting return loads and reduces their dependence on brokers.

    For shippers

    Shippers can see different transport options based on their routes. They can compare truck availability and capacity and negotiate directly with truck owners.

    Supply side

    We are initially targeting independent truck owners and small fleet operators.

    Demand side

    We are targeting small businesses, manufacturers, wholesalers and traders.
