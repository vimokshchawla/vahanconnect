VahanConnect
Trucks. Routes. Customers.

No truck should drive home empty.

After an interstate delivery, most trucks find no load back and return empty, losing the owner money on every round trip. VahanConnect matches those trucks with shippers on the same route, with verified owners and prices set by direct negotiation.
